Hello, world.

APR 07 2026 3 MIN READ #meta #site

I've started a lot of blogs. This one is different, I think, because it's boring on purpose.

It's a folder of HTML files on a server. No framework. No CMS. No database. No analytics. No JavaScript at all — which means nothing to track you, nothing to slow down the page, nothing that can break in three years when a dependency moves on.

To write a new post, I copy a template file, fill in the blanks, and push to git. The server pulls it. That's the whole system. I've been burned enough times by platforms that it feels almost radical to own the files outright.

What this will be

Loose notes on things I care about: surfing, coffee, the weird nostalgia of old games, Vietnam, whatever NASA is up to. Probably some longer essays when I have something worth saying.

I'm not going to commit to a posting schedule. That way lies guilt and abandoned projects. But I'll write when I have something to say, and I expect to have things to say.

On the design

The instinct is toward restraint. Warm tones, good type, nothing decorative that doesn't earn its place. There are sites out there doing this kind of thing beautifully — you can find a few on the about page.

The best websites are the ones that are still readable in ten years.

The color palette borrows from a few places at once: the burnt orange of NASA mission patches, the teal of Vietnamese lacquerware, the grey of a hotel corridor at 3am. It shouldn't work but somehow it does.

Anyway. Welcome. More soon.
