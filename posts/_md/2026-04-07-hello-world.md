## On the design

The instinct is toward restraint. Warm tones, good type, nothing
decorative that doesn't earn its place. There are sites out there
doing this kind of thing beautifully — you can find a few on
the [about page](../about.html).

The color palette borrows from a few places at once: the burnt orange
of NASA mission patches, the teal of Vietnamese lacquerware, the grey
of a hotel corridor at 3am.

Anyway. Welcome. More soon.
