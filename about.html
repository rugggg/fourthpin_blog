About

Interested in most things, expert in few.

I'm Doug. I make things and break things, usually in that order. Currently somewhere near the water, probably with a coffee.

Interests

Surfing — I've been chasing waves since I was a kid. Nothing else requires the same combination of patience, timing, and willingness to be humbled by something enormous and indifferent.

Coffee — Not as a lifestyle, more as a practice. The ritual of it. Slow mornings, a good grind, the smell before the first sip.

Video games — I grew up on them and never really stopped. Interested in games as a medium — what they can do that nothing else can.

Vietnam — I lived there for a while and it changed how I see things. The food, the heat, the noise, the way time moves differently there.

NASA — Aerospace, cosmology, the overview effect. The audacity of pointing a rocket at the void and trusting your math.

This site

A place to write and keep photos. The idea is that a website can be small, durable, and honest — and that those things are worth something.
